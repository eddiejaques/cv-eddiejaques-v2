01 — The Business Driver

A big budget needs proof of what it buys

Marketing wanted to spend on Samsung TVs the way it spends on phones: bid by bid, tuned on real subscriptions.

NEED

Know which TV ads create subscribers

Samsung Tizen TV was one of Joyn's largest untapped acquisition channels. The €15M budget only pays off if each campaign can be judged on downloads, streaming and paid sign-ups.

GAP

Standard tools don't see TVs

TVs have no browser cookies and no phone-style ad IDs. Samsung uses its own identifiers, so the usual tracking set-up has nothing to match on.

What was at stake
TV identifiers, not cookies = installs the usual tools can't match
€15M a year = every unmatched install is unmeasured spend
02 — The Obstacles

Three problems standing between the budget and the screen

Hardware limits, a closed ad network, and revenue that arrives after the ad.

HARDWARE

Low-spec TVs can't carry heavy tracking

Embedding a tracking SDK in the TV app slows it down and adds upkeep. Startup speed and playback stability had to stay intact.

SDK on a low-spec TV = slower app start, more maintenance
NETWORK

Samsung grades its own homework

Samsung Ads is a self-attributing network: it matches installs to its own ads. To optimise bids it needs Joyn to send back what happened after the install.

No postbacks = Samsung can't improve targeting or bids
REVENUE

Money arrives after the install

Installs and first opens are easy to count. Paid subscriptions and retention come later and sit in a separate database from the ad data.

Install-only tracking = campaign profit stays invisible
03 — The Solution

Move tracking off the TV and into the backend

Four steps: identify the TV, feed Samsung, respect consent, then join every ad to the revenue it produced. The track turns purple where the data moves from collection to answering the ROI question.

1
Send events from the backend TV → Joyn backend → Adjust

At app launch the TV's own advertising ID and its ad-tracking opt-out flag are read and linked to the Joyn account. Events then travel server to server into Adjust, signed with a security token so spoofed requests are rejected.

Adjust S2S · Samsung TIFA / PSID
2
Close the loop with Samsung Adjust → Samsung Ads

Samsung Ads was switched on as a self-attributing network inside Adjust. Installs, engagement and subscription events now flow to Samsung in real time, so its bidding and targeting learn from what Joyn actually cares about. A retry queue keeps events from being lost when the network drops.

SAN postbacks · Retry queue
3
Name the events, honour consent taxonomy + privacy

TV-specific moments such as first open, registration and 30 minutes of streaming were mapped to standard Adjust events. User consent choices travel with the data, so GDPR and Samsung's interest-based-ads opt-outs are respected automatically.

Event taxonomy · GDPR consent
4
Join ads to subscriptions Amazon Feeds → Snowflake → Tableau

Postbacks, click and impression logs and conversion data are pulled out through Amazon Feeds and loaded into Snowflake. There they are joined to the subscription database on the Joyn account ID. Tableau dashboards show campaign ROI, trial-to-paid conversion and 30, 60 and 90-day retention for Samsung spend.

Snowflake · Tableau · Amazon Feeds
