01 — The Context

Critical authentication gateway for healthcare services

HealthSafe ID is the single sign-on entry point for 60 million accounts across insurance, pharmacy, and financial services, where login failures directly impact patient access.

UNITEDHEALTHCARE

Insurance & claims access

Members authenticate to access insurance coverage, submit claims, and generate digital medical ID cards during care events.

Authentication failure = blocked access to care benefits
OPTUMRX

Prescription fulfillment

Gated home delivery prescription refills, where authentication drops cause medication shipment delays.

Login drops = delayed prescription fulfillment
OPTUM FINANCIAL

Health spending accounts

Managed HSA, FSA, and HRA account access tied to strict annual tax and reimbursement deadlines.

Access lockout = missed FSA and healthcare tax deadlines
02 — The Obstacles

Infrequent logins and legacy data fragmentation

Because members log in infrequently, credential forgetfulness is high. Concurrently migrating legacy portal credentials while traffic surged created massive data continuity risks.

REGISTRATION

Registration data mismatches

First-time onboarding failed when member demographic inputs conflicted with legacy enterprise records.

55–70% completion = high abandonment during onboarding
RECOVERY

Password recovery bottlenecks

Account recovery encountered MFA delivery timeouts, outdated mobile numbers, and validation deadlocks.

65–75% recovery = escalations to customer support
SESSION

Session token drop-outs

Returning user sessions failed due to corrupted browser state, token expiration, and legacy cookie handling.

80–85% success = unnecessary friction for active users
03 — The Solution

Real-time streaming telemetry on Adobe Experience Platform

We migrated identity tracking to Adobe Experience Platform Real-Time CDP, instrumenting HIPAA-compliant streaming telemetry across all three member portals.
