Case Study · Customer Engagement

Auditing a broken Segment-to-Intercom rollout down to two root causes.

Revenue Institute had wired Segment into Intercom across its sales platform, but live chat and product demo triggers kept failing in production. I audited the pipeline against all four acceptance criteria, isolated the broken user-sync flows, and documented the exact fixes for engineering.

Role Led the Intercom integration audit: traced identify calls, identity verification, and chat/demo failures across the Segment-to-Intercom pipeline

2013-2016 Intercom Engagement Platform Segment Cloud + Web destinations 4 acceptance criteria audited Mixpanel cross-checked
01 — The Business Driver

Sales needed live signup records, behavioral segments, and in-app chat

Revenue Institute sells training and software to B2B sales teams. Sales managers wanted Intercom to capture new signups immediately, segment them by product activity, and trigger live chat and product demo tours automatically.

IDENTIFY

Every signup should land in Intercom

New users on Revenue Institute needed an Intercom profile created the moment they signed up so sales reps had a reliable list of leads to contact.

No Intercom record on signup = sales has no lead to act on
SEGMENT

Managers need to slice users by behavior

Sales managers wanted to filter users by event history and profile traits so they could reach out with the right sales message at the right point in onboarding.

No usable segments = reps cannot target outreach by behavior
ENGAGE

Live chat and demos drive conversion

Logged-in users were supposed to see a live chat widget and an interactive product tour as primary conversion touchpoints inside the app.

Chat widget not rendering = primary in-app conversion channel stays dark
02 — The Obstacles

What the audit found broken in the pipeline

Three separate integration bugs (missing lifecycle identify calls, rejected API hashes, and blank company fields) were breaking the downstream features.

IDENTIFY CALLS

Users only synced on first signup

Intercom user creation fired during initial registration, but failed on page refreshes and subsequent logins because identify calls weren't triggered across the full session lifecycle.

Refresh and login don't sync = returning sessions stay invisible to Intercom
403 ERROR

Identity verification was blocking events

Intercom identity verification was turned on, but the backend wasn't passing a valid user_hash and the app domain wasn't allowlisted, causing 403 Forbidden errors on every event call.

Identity verification misconfigured = Intercom API rejects incoming events
COMPANY_ID

Chat and product tour required company fields

Both the live chat widget and product tours depended on company and company_id traits inside post-login identify payloads. Without them, Intercom rejected the calls with "company_id cannot be blank."

Blank company_id = chat widget and product tour fail to initialize
03 — The Solution

Tracing the pipeline from Segment payloads to the Intercom widget

I tested each acceptance criterion end to end, inspecting network payloads and Segment destination logs to pinpoint exactly where calls were dropping.

1
Audit user creation across the lifecycle signup → refresh → login

Tested identify calls across initial registration, page refresh, and repeat login, confirming that only registration fired properly while refresh and login dropped user context.Intercom · Identify calls

2
Verify Segment event mappings

Audited Segment's Intercom destination mappings (Auth, Identify, Account Email Verified, and Auth Login) and verified all four event streams were active for sales segmentation.Segment · Mappings

3
Isolate the 403 user_hash and company_id errors

Traced the missing chat widget to failed page and identify calls that lacked a valid user_hash, domain allowlisting, and non-null company / company_id traits.Identity verification · company_id

4
Deduplicate destinations and reconcile user counts

Documented how to route events cleanly through a single Segment connection mode and cross-checked unique user email counts between Mixpanel and Intercom.Segment destinations · Mixpanel
