Case Study · Product Analytics Enablement

Rebuilding mobile app analytics for ~19.5M Vodafone users.

Vodafone's legacy app tracking was over-reporting active usage by 30 to 40%, leaving product teams without an accurate baseline. Over a two-month sprint on-site in Cairo with a four-person mobile team, I rebuilt the tracking implementation on Adobe Analytics.

Role Led the mobile analytics rebuild: gap analysis, event taxonomy contracts, SDK integration, and validation

2017–2018 30–40% inflation corrected 36% digital adoption · ~19.5M 2 months timeline 5-person delivery team
01 — The Context

Distorted user metrics and unverified adoption numbers

Vodafone Egypt relied on mobile app penetration metrics to measure its digital transformation, but technical faults in the legacy tracking stack inflated numbers by up to 40%.

ACCURACY

30–40% metric inflation

Duplicate session initializations and faulty background event triggers systematically over-counted active app users.

Distorted metrics = inaccurate product and commercial decisions
BENCHMARKING

No verified performance baseline

Without clean telemetry, product managers couldn't accurately measure feature adoption, drop-offs, or retention cohorts.

No clean baseline = unable to evaluate release impact
EXPERIMENTATION

Inability to run targeted tests

The legacy telemetry structure lacked user-property attributes required to power segmentation or targeted A/B tests.

No behavioral segmentation = generic app experiences for all users
02 — The Obstacles

Tight release windows and distributed engineering

Correcting the tracking required standardizing data schemas across iOS and Android and coordinating directly with engineering inside an aggressive sprint schedule.

TAXONOMY

No standard event contracts

iOS and Android apps recorded interactions differently, creating schema fragmentation that broke centralized reporting.

Disjointed schemas = inconsistent cross-platform reporting
COLLABORATION

Cross-team coordination barrier

Remote ticket handoffs were causing misinterpretations of SDK requirements and delaying release schedules.

Remote disconnect = needed embedded on-site technical guidance
TIMELINE

Fixed two-month go-live deadline

Audit, taxonomy definition, SDK implementation, QA, and production deployment had to complete within 60 days.

60-day runway = zero room for development iteration delays
03 — The Solution

Embedded delivery, standardized taxonomy, and Adobe Analytics cutover

Working directly on-site with the 4-developer mobile engineering team in Cairo, we defined schema contracts, implemented the Adobe Mobile SDK, and validated event payloads.

1
Telemetry audit & gap identification audit → payload validation
