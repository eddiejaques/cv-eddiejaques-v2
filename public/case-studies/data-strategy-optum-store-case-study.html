Case Study · Measurement Strategy

Designing the launch KPI framework for Optum Store.

When Optum Store launched to sell generic prescriptions online, it had no historical benchmarks. I designed the measurement plan and weekly dashboard suite used by executives, product managers, category owners, and marketing.

Role Led the measurement strategy: KPI framework design, funnel event taxonomy, dashboard development, and stakeholder rollout

2018–2020 4 stakeholder groups served Year 1 baseline metrics established Weekly & monthly dashboards Orders, traffic & funnel KPIs
01 — The Context

Greenfield eCommerce launch without performance baselines

Optum Store was created to provide direct-to-consumer generic medications with HSA and insurance integration at checkout, launching without established conversion baselines.

COMPETITION

Direct-to-consumer pharmacy model

Entering the generic drug market required competing with established digital pharmacies and retail healthcare players.

No historical baseline = impossible to evaluate launch performance
CHECKOUT

Complex healthcare checkout journeys

Purchases involved HSA cards, prescription validation, and standard payments, requiring multi-layered journey tracking.

Multi-step validation = high checkout drop-off risk
ALIGNMENT

Disconnected reporting needs

Marketing, product, category managers, and executives needed different cuts of data from a single tracking system.

Fragmented requirements = risk of siloed, conflicting numbers
02 — The Obstacles

Unified definitions across four distinct stakeholder groups

The tracking model had to satisfy operational product teams needing daily funnel diagnostics while giving executive leadership clean high-level KPIs.

AUDIENCE

Diverse analytical requirements

CXOs focused on revenue and customer acquisition cost; category leads needed SKU-level conversion and inventory velocity.

4 distinct audiences = one data model had to serve all views
LIFECYCLE

Full lifecycle visibility

Metrics had to track users from initial paid/organic acquisition through repeat prescription refills and basket size growth.

End-to-end journey = isolated web page analytics were insufficient
CADENCE

Automated reporting cadences

Operational reviews required weekly automated dashboards, complemented by monthly executive summary rollups.

Recurring reporting = automated ETL and dashboard pipelines required
03 — The Solution

KPI hierarchy, data layer taxonomy, and executive dashboard suite

We built an end-to-end measurement hierarchy connecting raw event tracking to business outcomes and surfaced them in tailored dashboards.

1
